DEAR FRANCOIS, I WAS VERY DISTRESSED TO HEAR THAT YOUR LATEST BEAUTIFUL PORTRAIT OF ME CANNOT BE USED FOR THE FOYER DISPLAY AT OUR MATINEE PERFORMANCE NEXT WEEK. THE SITTING WAS YOUR BEST WORK AND REALLY CAPTURED THE AURA OF THE PLAY. THE PRINCE OF WALES WOULD HAVE LOVED IT, AND I AM SURE HE WILL BE AS CROSS AS I AM. IT IS REALLY TOO BAD. THIS IS, I THINK, THE SIXTH PHOTOGRAPHIC PLATE TO HAVE CRACKED IN ONLY TWO MONTHS, AND I CAN ONLY THINK THAT YOUR SUPPLIER IS STORING THEM CARELESSLY, LEADING TO A WEAKNESS. AS YOU KNOW I WAS HOPING TO CONVINCE ALBERT THAT WE SHOULD TAKE THE PLAY TO LONDON FOR A SEASON, AND THE THEATRE HAS WORKED SO VERY HARD TO IMPRESS HIM. IS THERE NO CHANCE THAT YOU COULD RECOVER THE IMAGE, OR PERHAPS, ANOTHER THAT WE COULD USE INSTEAD FOR THE DECORATIONS? AND WILL YOU PROMISE ME THAT YOU WILL INVESTIGATE THE CAUSE OF THE BREAKAGES? WHILE I ADORE SITTING FOR YOU, IT IS VERY HARD TO FIND THE TIME WE NEED TO DO THAT PROPERLY WHILE TAKING CARE OF ALL THE MINUTIAE INVOLVED IN THE DAY TO DAY RUNNING OF THE SHOW, AND IF THE PRINCE IS AMENABLE TO MY PROPOSAL TO RETURN TO LONDON, THEN THERE WILL BE EVEN MORE TO ARRANGE. DEAR FRANCOIS, I BEG YOU TO PLEASE AT LEAST TRY TO RESCUE THE IMAGE. BEFORE I LEAVE THIS LETTER, ALLOW ME TO BEG ANOTHER FAVOUR. I HAVE FOUND ANOTHER OF THOSE CURIOUS ENCRYPTED MESSAGES AMONG THE ADVERTISEMENTS IN THE NEWSPAPER. I REALLY DO THINK IT WOULD BE FUN TO LEARN TO DECIPHER THEM THE WAY YOU DO, SO, AS YOU CAN SEE, I HAVE BEGUN TO LEARN, ENCRYPTING THIS MESSAGE WITH A SIMPLE CAESAR SHIFT CIPHER TO DEMONSTRATE MY NEWFOUND SKILLS. I HOPE YOU ENJOY BREAKING THE CODE AS MUCH AS I DID USING IT! YOUR LOVING FRIEND, SARAH
